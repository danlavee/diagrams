# Composition

Opened from SKILL.md.

Composition places nodes so **every fact the picture must keep holding** can hold. Pathways get better because grouping moved.

A fact the picture must keep holding is a relationship on the lowest parent that contains the participants (or nesting). Domain meaning is not enough. How marks sit, occupy, meet, and are read is owed whether or not the storyboard named it. Composition is not finished while a visible picture fact has no home.

A home is a relationship that grouping can discharge without leftover. Naming leftover is not a home.

**Sameness.** Marks the picture treats as one peer set share that set's geometric attributes. Flow shares the picture's reading unless a relationship names another direction.

**Meeting.** A path ends on one meeting. The receiver determines that place. Several meetings on one receiver are a peer set of that receiver.

**Kind.** Kind is discharged by the coarsest marks that still read as that node and hold its meetings. Internals of a node are not a picture unless the storyboard is those internals.

**Leftover.** Geometry that sameness, meeting, and kind do not determine is leftover. Extra paint at a meeting is leftover unless more than two same-path ends join there. Re-enter composition. If leftover still cannot be discharged on one surface, the storyboard is more than one picture.

## How to use

1. Place groups from the storyboard so sameness, meeting, and kind can hold.
2. Write every picture-holding relationship on the parent that owns those participants. Write a relationship when the story names an exception to sameness or meeting. Kind has no relationship-exception: internals are a picture only when the storyboard is those internals.
3. If they cannot be discharged without leftover, return to step 1. If leftover still cannot be discharged on one surface, the storyboard is more than one picture — stop this composition; do not keep adding bends.
4. Paint only after they can be discharged onto marks. Paint is the coarsest marks that still read as each node's kind and hold its meetings.

The seeing pass looks at the marks. Sameness and meeting still hold, or a relationship names the story's exception. Kind still holds as coarsest marks; a relationship cannot except kind. Every other visible picture fact has a relationship. No leftover remains. If not, grouping or the ledger is incomplete — return to composition, not more routing. If leftover cannot be discharged on one surface, this is not one picture.

## When the picture has paths

Same law. A same-path meeting is a joint; clearance from that joint to the next meeting is a relationship. Fan-out is one trunk then branches of the same path; fan-in is the reverse. The same path may join at a joint; distinct paths do not share a segment. Two paths at one point are a joint only if they are the same path; otherwise they cross or composition moves them — that choice is a relationship.
