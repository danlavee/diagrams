# Composition

Opened from SKILL.md.

Composition places nodes so **every fact the picture must keep holding** can hold. Pathways get better because grouping moved.

A fact the picture must keep holding is a relationship on the lowest parent that contains the participants (or nesting). Domain meaning is not enough. Composition is not finished while a visible picture fact has no home.

If discharging a relationship would add a bend, put a mark on another mark, merge what the story did not join, or force a meeting the relationships do not settle, **re-enter composition**. Do not keep adding bends.

## How to use

1. Place groups from the storyboard.
2. Write every picture-holding relationship on the parent that owns those participants.
3. If they cannot be discharged without leftover geometry the story did not name — extra joint, occupancy fight, illegal merge, off-normal landing, or an unsettled meeting of any marks — return to step 1.
4. Paint only after they can be discharged onto marks.

The seeing pass looks for those facts on the marks. If a fact is visible and has no relationship, the ledger is incomplete — return to composition, not more routing.

## When the picture has paths

Same law. A same-path meeting is a joint; clearance from that joint to the next meeting is a relationship. A path meets a face along the normal, or a vertex only when the story names it. Fan-out is one trunk then branches of the same path; fan-in is the reverse. The same path may join at a joint; distinct paths do not share a segment. Two paths at one point are a joint only if they are the same path; otherwise they cross or composition moves them — that choice is a relationship.
